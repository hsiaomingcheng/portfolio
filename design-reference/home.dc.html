ABOUT

From Taiwan to Canterbury

I spent seven years as a frontend developer in Taiwan, building commercial websites and maintaining live sites for clients ranging from a hotel, a jewellery brand and a tyre company to a food R&D company, a game developer and Taipei's Museum of Contemporary Art. I then moved to New Zealand to study a Master of Applied Computing at Lincoln University, where I've grown into full-stack work and building with AI.

At Frizzell I learned that code is only half the job. As the only IT person, I gathered requirements, planned the timeline and explained technical choices in plain language to a non-technical team.

How I work

  • Break big jobs into small steps, and check each one before moving on
  • Use AI tools to move faster — then review every line myself
  • Explain the "why" behind technical decisions, not just the "what"
  • Prefer working on-site: quick conversations beat long chat threads
